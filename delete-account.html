ArenaX - Account Deletion

ArenaX respects your privacy. You can request deletion of your ArenaX account and associated personal data by following the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support email address.
  2. Use the subject "ArenaX Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address registered with your ArenaX account.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership.

Click here to request account deletion

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account.
  • Registered email address.
  • Username and profile information.
  • Profile photo.
  • User-created posts and captions.
  • Uploaded post images.
  • Associated likes and comments, where applicable.

Data Retention

After verifying your request, we aim to delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

Certain information may be retained if required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Such information will only be retained for the period necessary.
