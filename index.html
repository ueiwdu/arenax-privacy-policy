ArenaX – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

ArenaX respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share information when you use ArenaX.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email Address: Used for account identification and authentication.
  • Username: Used to identify users within ArenaX.
  • Profile Photo: Used to display your profile image.
  • User-Generated Content: Captions and images uploaded when creating posts.
  • Technical Information: Limited technical information may be processed by our application and service providers to maintain security and functionality.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage accounts.
  • To display usernames and profile photos.
  • To allow users to create and share posts.
  • To maintain application functionality and security.
  • To respond to support requests.

4. Data Storage and Services

ArenaX uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication (where enabled) and Cloud Firestore, to support account management and data storage.

Profile photos and post images may be compressed, encoded in Base64 format and stored in Firestore as part of user profile and post records.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by Google Firebase to provide application services. Usernames, profile photos and posts may be visible to other ArenaX users. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect users and the application.
